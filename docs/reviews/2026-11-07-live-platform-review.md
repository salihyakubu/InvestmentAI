### Quant researcher — CONCERN: no live edge claim is made today, but two surfaces can manufacture one

Mechanics: the per-quarter "mean" is the mean over 8h stamps of a per-stamp cross-sectional Spearman IC between the factor and the demeaned 24h forward return on all live USDT perps (≥20), gross of costs (main services/research/funding_watch.py:489-504). n counts stamps, not symbol-stamps (:764; api/routers/research.py:130). "k/4" is `current_positive_streak`, walked from the latest bucket of `quarterly_rollup`, which INCLUDES the open calendar quarter (research.py:115-120); `positive` is `mean > 0` with no t and no minimum n (funding_watch.py:767). The verdict string promises "complete quarters" (research.py:145-148); the number does not deliver it (ResearchWatch.tsx:58).
- S1, CONFIRMED: the readiness gate is passable by a null factor. frontend/src/components/settings/GoLiveReadiness.tsx:45-51 passes on ANY one of 14 with streak ≥ 4.
